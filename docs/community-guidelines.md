# Bingle Community Guidelines

**Version 1** · Draft pending legal review

Bingle is for private conversations between people who want to talk to each other. These guidelines describe what is not allowed. They are part of the [Bingle Terms of Use](../terms/), and breaking them can get you blocked from the Bingle network.

Bingle messages are end-to-end encrypted, so we cannot see them. We rely on users to report messages that break these guidelines. Moderators review every report.

## Not allowed

**Harassment or bullying.** Do not send repeated unwanted messages, insults, degrading comments or content intended to shame or intimidate someone. If someone blocks you or asks you to stop, stop.

**Hate speech.** Do not attack people on the basis of race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, disability or serious disease.

**Violence or credible threats.** Do not threaten to harm anyone, encourage violence, or celebrate violent attacks.

**Nudity or sexual content.** Do not send sexual images or messages to anyone who has not clearly agreed to receive them.

**Sexual exploitation.** Never share, request or promote content that sexually exploits anyone, including non-consensual intimate images. Any sexual content involving a minor is strictly forbidden: we block the identity and refer the content to law enforcement.

**Suicide or self-injury.** Do not encourage or instruct anyone to harm themselves. If you or someone you are talking to is at risk, contact local emergency services or a crisis line.

**Spam.** Do not send bulk, repetitive or unsolicited commercial messages.

**Scams or fraud.** Do not deceive people to obtain money, credentials, personal information or access to accounts.

**Impersonation.** Do not pretend to be another person, organisation or Bingle moderator.

**Restricted or illegal goods.** Do not offer to buy, sell or trade illegal drugs, weapons or other goods or services whose sale is illegal.

## Reporting

To report a message, long-press it in the chat and choose **Report**. Choose the reason that fits best, and add details if they help. Reports are sent to Bingle moderators together with the message's cryptographic signature, which proves who sent it.

Report honestly. Filing reports you know to be false, or using reports to harass someone, breaks the [Terms of Use](../terms/).

## What happens next

Moderators review each report against these guidelines. If a message breaks them, we may block the sender's identity from the Bingle network and stop them registering a new one. Where the law requires it, or someone is at risk of harm, we refer content to the authorities.
