# Bingle Terms of Use

**Version 1** · Draft pending legal review

These Terms of Use apply to your use of the Bingle messaging app ("Bingle"), published by the Bingle Foundation ("we", "us"). They apply in addition to the licence under which you obtained the app. For App Store downloads, that is Apple's [Standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/).

By tapping **Agree** in the app, you accept these terms. If you do not accept them, do not use Bingle.

## 1. How Bingle works

Bingle is a peer-to-peer, end-to-end encrypted messenger. Your messages travel directly between devices, or are held in encrypted form until the recipient collects them. We cannot read your messages. Moderators see a message only when a user reports it, and then only what that user chooses to include in the report.

## 2. Zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive users

You must follow the [Bingle Community Guidelines](../community-guidelines/). There is **no tolerance** for objectionable content or abusive behaviour. In particular, you must not use Bingle to send:

- harassment, bullying, threats or incitement to violence;
- hate speech;
- sexual content sent without consent, or any sexual content involving a minor;
- content promoting suicide or self-injury;
- spam, scams or fraud;
- content that impersonates another person;
- offers of illegal or restricted goods or services;
- material that infringes copyright or other intellectual property rights;
- any other communication that is unlawful.

Bingle may perform automated checks of messages on your local device before they are sent, and stop those containing offensive language.

## 3. Reporting

You can report a message you receive from within the app. A report sends the message, its sender's cryptographic signature and, if you agree, the surrounding conversation to Bingle moderators.

**Filing a report you know to be false, or filing reports maliciously or to harass another user, is a breach of these terms.**

## 4. Enforcement

If we find that a user has broken these terms or the Community Guidelines, we may, without notice:

- block their identity from the Bingle network, so they can no longer send or receive messages through it;
- prevent them from registering a new handle or identity to get around a block;
- refer content to law enforcement where the law requires it or where someone is at risk of harm. Content involving the sexual exploitation of a minor is always referred.

You can also block a user yourself from within the app, so that you stop receiving their messages.

## 5. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. When a change materially affects your rights or obligations, we increase the version number above and the app asks you to accept the new version before you can continue using it.

## 6. Contact

Questions about these terms or the Community Guidelines: open an issue at [github.com/binglefoundation/bingle_rust](https://github.com/binglefoundation/bingle_rust/issues), or report a message from within the app.
